Titus is a farmer.
He lives not in the city but in the country.
The farm is small, but the land is good.
He works in the fields every day.
For the work is long and hard.
And yet Titus is happy.
He loves his farm and his oxen.
The oxen are strong but slow.
So Titus walks slowly behind the oxen.
His wife Claudia works in the house.
She is not lazy but industrious.
The small farm provides food for the family.
Their son Lucius is small,
but he carries water from the river.
Hot is the sun, and long is the road.
Lucius is tired, for the water is heavy.
"Oh Jupiter, the day is hot!"
"Oh Ceres, give us a good harvest!"
Titus often prays to the gods.
For the gods give rain and sun.
The farmer is poor, and yet he is free.
He has no master.
So he is often content.
In the evening the family eats bread and cheese.
The bread is hard, but the cheese is good.
After dinner Titus sits in front of the house.
He looks at the stars and is silent.
"Oh Mars, protect my fields!"
The work of the farm never ends.
The fields of the farmer are wide and green.
Titus is old, but he is still strong.
He does not work in the town but on his own land.
The land of the farmer is not large, but it is enough.
For he is self-sufficient.
And yet the winter is long and cold.
In winter the oxen stay in the stable.
"Oh Minerva, help my wife!"
Claudia is wise, so the family is happy.
The life of a farmer is hard,
but it is good and free.
